Install in under a minute

Your store, one tap away.

Lovable Store installs straight from the live site as a web app. It opens fullscreen with its own icon, keeps your bag and wishlist close, and works offline for browsing.

  1. 1 Open the live store in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone.
  2. 2 Tap Install app, or choose Add to Home Screen from the share menu.
  3. 3 Launch Lovable Store from your home screen — it opens like a native app.
